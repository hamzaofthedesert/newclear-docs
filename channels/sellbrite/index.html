Sellbrite

Sellbrite is a multi-channel listing and inventory management platform that connects to a wide range of marketplaces. By connecting Newclear to Sellbrite, you can extend your reach to channels that Newclear doesn't integrate with directly.

When to Use Sellbrite​

Use Sellbrite if you need to sell on channels beyond Newclear's direct integrations — for example, Etsy, Newegg, or other regional marketplaces. Sellbrite acts as a bridge: Newclear pushes to Sellbrite, Sellbrite pushes to those additional channels.

tip

If you already use Sellbrite and have existing listings there, connecting Newclear to Sellbrite lets you consolidate inventory management in one place (Newclear) while keeping your Sellbrite channel relationships.

Getting Your Sellbrite API Key​

  1. Log in to your Sellbrite account at sellbrite.com
  2. Go to Settings > API
  3. Copy your API Key (also called Account Token)
  4. Copy your API Secret Key if required

Connecting Sellbrite in Newclear​

  1. Go to Settings > Channels > Sellbrite
  2. Click Connect Sellbrite
  3. Enter your API Key (and Secret if required)
  4. Click Save & Verify

Newclear will confirm the connection with a success message.

Sync Behavior​

Once connected, Newclear syncs to Sellbrite as follows:

  • Products — Active products in Newclear are pushed to Sellbrite as listings
  • Inventory — Quantity changes in Newclear are pushed to Sellbrite, which then updates inventory across all channels in your Sellbrite account
  • Orders — Orders from Sellbrite-connected channels are imported back into Newclear

Order Import​

Orders from Sellbrite's connected channels flow into Newclear's Orders section. They're tagged with the originating channel (e.g., Etsy, Newegg) so you can distinguish them.

Tips​

warning

If you use Sellbrite as an intermediary, be aware that inventory changes on Sellbrite-connected channels flow back through Sellbrite to Newclear. This can sometimes cause a 15–30 minute delay. For time-sensitive inventory (low stock items), monitor the stats closely.

note

Sellbrite has its own subscription fee separate from Newclear. Connecting to Sellbrite doesn't include a Sellbrite subscription — you need your own active Sellbrite account.
