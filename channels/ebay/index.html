eBay

Newclear's eBay integration lets you push Fixed Price listings directly from your catalog, keep inventory synced, and pull orders back — all without logging into eBay Seller Hub. For auto parts sellers, fitment data is automatically injected into listings.

Connecting eBay​

  1. Go to Settings > Channels > eBay
  2. Click Connect eBay
  3. You'll be redirected to eBay's OAuth authorization page
  4. Log in with your eBay seller account and click Agree
  5. You'll return to Newclear with the connection active
note

Make sure you're logging into the correct eBay account. If you have multiple eBay seller accounts, connect the one you want to list products under.

Pushing Products as Fixed Price Listings​

Newclear pushes eBay listings as Fixed Price (Buy It Now) format. Auction-style listings are not supported.

For a product to successfully push to eBay:

  • Status must be Active
  • Product must have a Category with a valid eBay mapping (see Categories)
  • Must have at least one image
  • Price must be set
  • Quantity must be > 0

Products that meet all requirements are pushed automatically when the channel is connected. You can also manually push from the product's Channels tab.

Category Mapping​

Every Newclear category must be mapped to an eBay category ID. Newclear attempts this automatically via AI when you create a category.

If a product isn't listing because of a category error, check:

  1. Go to Settings > Categories
  2. Find your category
  3. Confirm the eBay mapping status is AI Mapped or Manual (not Unmapped or Failed)

👉 See Categories for how mapping works.

Auto Parts Fitment Injection​

If a product is in an Auto Parts category and has fitment rows (Year/Make/Model/Trim/Engine), Newclear automatically includes an ItemCompatibilityList in the eBay XML when listing or updating.

This allows eBay buyers to search for parts by their specific vehicle. Listings with fitment data show a "Fits your vehicle" badge to buyers.

👉 See Fitment & eBay Listings for full details.

eBay Stats Bar​

The eBay channel page shows a stats bar with four counters:

StatusMeaning
LiveActively listed on eBay and visible to buyers
ErrorListing failed or has an issue — click to see the error message
On HoldListing paused (e.g., out of stock, manually paused, or channel disconnected)
OrphanedThe eBay listing exists but Newclear has lost track of the link (e.g., the Newclear product was deleted)

Viewing Live eBay Listings​

From the Products page (Channels tab) or the eBay channel dashboard, you can click the eBay Item ID number next to a listing. This opens the live eBay listing in a new tab so you can see exactly what buyers see.

Interpreting Errors​

When a listing has Error status, click on it to see the error message from eBay. Common errors:

  • "Condition is not supported" — The condition you selected isn't valid for that eBay category. Check eBay's requirements for the category
  • "Missing required item specifics" — The eBay category requires additional fields (like Size or Color for clothing). Add these to the product details
  • "Price too low" — eBay has minimum price requirements for some categories
  • "Category not found" — The eBay category mapping is invalid. Re-map the category in Settings

Re-listing On-Hold Items​

If a listing is On Hold (e.g., it went out of stock and was paused):

  1. Add stock back to the product (update quantity > 0)
  2. The listing should automatically resume on the next sync cycle
  3. Or manually trigger from the product's Channels tab

Disconnecting eBay​

  1. Go to Settings > Channels > eBay
  2. Click Disconnect
warning

Disconnecting eBay does not end your live eBay listings. They stay active until they sell, expire, or you end them manually in eBay Seller Hub. Newclear will stop syncing inventory and orders after disconnection.

Tips​

tip

Check the Error count on the eBay stats bar regularly — even one or two errors can mean missed sales. Fix them promptly by addressing the error message shown.

tip

eBay's Item Specifics requirements vary by category. For best results with clothing: always set Size, Color, Brand, and Material. For electronics: always set Brand, Model, MPN, and Connectivity. These improve listing quality and buyer trust.
