Shopify

Newclear's Shopify integration keeps your product catalog and inventory in sync with your Shopify store in real time. Push products from Newclear to Shopify, and orders placed on Shopify flow back automatically.

Connecting Shopify​

  1. Go to Settings > Channels > Shopify
  2. Click Connect Shopify
  3. Enter your Shopify store URL (e.g., your-store.myshopify.com)
  4. Click Authorize — you'll be redirected to Shopify's OAuth page
  5. Review the permissions Newclear needs and click Install
  6. You'll be redirected back to Newclear with Shopify connected

The OAuth flow grants Newclear access to manage products, inventory, and read orders on your Shopify store.

note

You need to be a Shopify store owner or have permissions to install apps. If you're not the owner, ask them to complete the connection.

Pushing Products to Shopify​

After connecting, push your products:

Automatic push: Products with Status = Active are automatically pushed to Shopify once the connection is established. New active products added to Newclear are pushed within minutes.

Manual push:

  1. Open a product in Newclear
  2. Click the Channels tab
  3. Find Shopify in the list
  4. Click Push to Shopify

Inventory Sync — Newclear is Master​

Newclear owns the inventory number. When stock changes in Newclear (from any source — POS sale, channel order, manual adjustment, POW receive), the updated quantity is pushed to Shopify immediately.

warning

Do not manually adjust inventory quantities in Shopify. Newclear will overwrite them on the next sync cycle. All inventory changes should happen in Newclear.

Pulling Orders from Shopify​

Shopify orders sync to Newclear via webhooks — this is real-time sync. When a customer places an order on your Shopify store:

  1. Shopify sends a webhook to Newclear within seconds
  2. Newclear creates the order record
  3. Inventory is decremented for each item in the order
  4. The order appears in your Orders list

If a webhook is missed (rare), Newclear also polls Shopify periodically to catch any gaps.

Disconnecting Shopify​

  1. Go to Settings > Channels > Shopify
  2. Click Disconnect
  3. Confirm
warning

Disconnecting Shopify doesn't remove your products from Shopify — they stay live. But Newclear will stop syncing inventory and orders. Make sure you have a plan for managing your Shopify store independently before disconnecting.

Troubleshooting​

  • Products not appearing in Shopify: Check that the product Status is Active (not Draft) and the product has been saved after the Shopify connection was made
  • Orders not syncing: Check webhook configuration in Shopify (Settings > Notifications > Webhooks) — Newclear's webhook URL should be listed
  • Inventory out of sync: Manually trigger a sync from Settings > Channels > Shopify > Resync Inventory

👉 See Troubleshooting Channel Sync for more solutions.
