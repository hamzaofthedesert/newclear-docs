Layaway

Layaway lets customers reserve items by paying a deposit upfront, then completing payment over time before taking the items home. Newclear's layaway system tracks deposits, balances, and payment history for each layaway plan.

What Layaway Is​

A layaway plan:

  • Holds specific products for a customer
  • Requires a partial deposit to start (you set how much)
  • Allows additional payments over time
  • Items are not released until the full balance is paid
  • Inventory is reserved (won't sell to someone else) for the duration

Creating a Layaway​

  1. Go to POS and click the Layaway tab
  2. Click New Layaway
  3. Search for and add the product(s) the customer wants to reserve
  4. Look up or create a Customer — layaways require a customer record
  5. Set the Deposit Amount (minimum deposit, if you have a policy)
  6. Collect the deposit from the customer using any payment method
  7. Click Create Layaway

The layaway is now active. The product(s) are flagged as reserved. A receipt or confirmation can be printed for the customer.

tip

Set a minimum deposit policy (e.g., 20% of total) and communicate it clearly to customers. You can enforce this manually — Newclear doesn't restrict the deposit amount.

Finding an Existing Layaway​

  1. Go to POS > Layaway tab
  2. All active layaway plans are listed with:
    • Customer name
    • Items reserved
    • Total amount
    • Amount paid so far
    • Balance remaining
    • Date created
  3. Click on a layaway to open it

You can also search by customer name.

Adding Payments to a Layaway​

When a customer makes a payment toward their layaway:

  1. Find the layaway in the Layaway tab
  2. Click Add Payment
  3. Select payment method (Cash, Card, CashApp/Zelle)
  4. Enter the payment amount (can be any amount up to the remaining balance)
  5. Confirm

The payment is recorded and the balance updates. A receipt can be given for each payment.

Completing a Layaway​

When the customer pays the full balance:

  1. Open the layaway
  2. Add the final payment
  3. The system recognizes the balance is now $0
  4. Click Complete or Release Items
  5. The sale is finalized, inventory is deducted, and a final receipt is generated

The layaway moves to completed status.

Voiding a Layaway​

If a customer cancels or abandons their layaway:

  1. Open the layaway
  2. Click Void or Cancel Layaway
  3. Confirm
warning

Voiding a layaway does not automatically refund deposits. You'll need to manually process any refund for payments already made. Decide on your layaway cancellation/refund policy before offering layaway to customers.

After voiding, the reserved products become available for sale again.

Layaway and Inventory​

When a layaway is created, the products are reserved in Newclear. Reserved items:

  • Still appear in your inventory count (they haven't moved)
  • Won't be offered for sale via POS (flagged as reserved)
  • May still appear on online channels depending on your settings

When the layaway is completed, inventory is decremented (same as a regular sale).

Tips​

tip

Always create a customer profile for every layaway. This ensures you have contact info to reach the customer about their remaining balance.

note

Layaway refunds appear as a discrepancy type in the EOD report under "Layaway Refund." See End-of-Day Report.
