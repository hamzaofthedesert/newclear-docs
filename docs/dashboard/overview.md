# Dashboard Overview

The Newclear Dashboard is the first thing you see when you log in. It gives you a live, at-a-glance summary of your business — active products, recent sales, pending deliveries, and inventory alerts — all in one place.

The dashboard is fully widget-based, meaning you can show or hide any section based on what's most relevant to your role and workflow.

---

## Dashboard Layout

The dashboard is divided into a responsive grid of **widget cards**. Each widget displays a specific metric or chart. Widgets are arranged automatically based on your screen size and your current widget preferences.

The top of the dashboard shows a **greeting** with your user name and today's date. Below that, your enabled widgets are displayed in order.

---

## Available Widgets

Newclear ships with **12 configurable dashboard widgets**. Here's what each one shows:

### 📦 Active Products
Displays the total number of products currently in **Active** status in your catalog. This is a quick health check on your published inventory.

### 📊 Orders Chart
A visual bar or line chart showing order volume over time (daily or weekly). Useful for spotting trends and peak selling periods.

### 🛒 POS Sale Statistics
Summarizes your Point of Sale activity — total POS transactions, total POS revenue, and average sale value for the selected period.

### 📈 Sales Chart
A high-level revenue chart combining all sales channels (POS + channel orders). Lets you visualize total business performance over time.

### ⏳ Pending Sales Payments
Lists sales where payment has not yet been collected or confirmed. This widget helps you stay on top of outstanding balances so nothing slips through the cracks.

### 🚚 Pending Sales Delivery
Shows orders that have been paid for but not yet fulfilled or shipped. Helps your fulfillment team prioritize what needs to go out the door.

### 🔔 Reorder Alerts
Highlights products that have fallen below their configured reorder threshold. Click any item to go directly to its inventory record. Keeping this widget visible helps prevent stockouts.

---

## Additional Widgets (Configurable)

The following widgets can also be enabled from Dashboard Preferences:

- **Total Revenue** — Cumulative revenue for the current period
- **New Customers** — Count of customers added in the current period
- **Top Selling Products** — Your best performers by units sold
- **Low Stock Warning** — Products approaching but not yet at reorder threshold
- **Recent Activity Feed** — A log of recent actions in the system

---

## Enabling & Disabling Widgets

You can customize exactly which widgets appear on your dashboard:

1. Click your **profile avatar** or go to **Settings → Preferences → Dashboard**
2. You'll see a list of all available widgets with toggle switches
3. Toggle any widget **on** or **off**
4. Changes are saved automatically and take effect immediately

:::tip
Different team members can have different widget configurations. A warehouse manager might want Reorder Alerts and Pending Delivery front and center, while a sales manager might prefer the Sales Chart and POS Statistics.
:::

---

## Refreshing the Dashboard

The dashboard auto-refreshes when you navigate to it. If you've been on the dashboard for a while and want fresh data, simply click the browser's refresh button or navigate away and back.

---

## Dashboard vs. Reports

The dashboard is designed for **quick daily monitoring** — it gives you real-time snapshots, not deep dives. For detailed analysis (date range filters, export to CSV, grouped reports), use the **Reports** section under Sales & Finance.

:::note
Widgets only show data you have permission to view. If a widget appears blank, your user role may not have access to that data type. Contact your Super Admin to adjust permissions.
:::
