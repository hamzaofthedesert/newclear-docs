Shipday Integration Reactor

The Shipday Integration Reactor connects Newclear with Shipday — a last-mile delivery management platform. Once connected, you can dispatch orders for delivery directly from Newclear, track drivers in real time, and provide customers with live delivery updates.

What Shipday Does​

Shipday is a delivery dispatch and tracking platform that:

  • Accepts delivery orders and assigns them to drivers
  • Provides a driver mobile app (iOS/Android) for turn-by-turn navigation
  • Offers real-time tracking links for customers
  • Generates proof of delivery (photo, signature)
  • Provides a dispatcher dashboard

The Newclear Reactor integrates Newclear orders directly into Shipday's dispatch system.

Prerequisites​

  • A Shipday account (Free plan available; paid for advanced features)
  • A Shipday API Key from your Shipday dashboard

Getting Your Shipday API Key​

  1. Log into your Shipday account at app.shipday.com
  2. Go to Settings → API
  3. Copy your API Key

Connecting Shipday to Newclear​

  1. Install the Shipday Integration Reactor from Settings → Reactors
  2. Go to Settings → Reactors → Shipday
  3. Paste your Shipday API Key
  4. Click Test Connection
  5. On success, click Save

Dispatching an Order for Delivery​

Once Shipday is connected, you can send any Newclear order to Shipday for dispatch:

From an Order​

  1. Open the order in Orders section
  2. Click Dispatch for Delivery
  3. Fill in the delivery details:
    • Customer Address — Pre-filled from the order; edit if needed
    • Delivery Date/Time — Scheduled delivery window
    • Driver — Assign a specific driver (optional; can auto-assign)
    • Notes — Special delivery instructions
  4. Click Send to Shipday

The order is created in Shipday as a delivery job. The assigned driver receives a notification on their Shipday Driver app.

Bulk Dispatch​

From the Orders list:

  1. Filter by "Ready for Delivery"
  2. Select multiple orders
  3. Bulk Actions → Dispatch to Shipday
  4. Set a delivery date and confirm

Tracking Deliveries​

After dispatch:

  1. On the order detail in Newclear, a Tracking section appears
  2. Shows current delivery status: Pending → Picked Up → In Transit → Delivered
  3. A customer tracking link is generated by Shipday — you can send this to the customer
  4. Estimated delivery time updates in real time

Delivery Statuses​

StatusMeaning
DispatchedOrder sent to Shipday, awaiting driver
AssignedDriver assigned
Picked UpDriver has collected the order
In TransitEn route to customer
DeliveredConfirmed delivery
FailedDelivery attempt failed

Proof of Delivery​

When a driver completes a delivery in the Shipday app:

  • They can capture a photo and/or digital signature
  • These are stored in Shipday and linked to the Newclear order
  • View proof of delivery from the order detail page

Shipday and POS Orders​

POS orders with a delivery component can also be dispatched:

  1. After completing a POS sale with delivery arrangement
  2. Open the order from Sales History or Orders
  3. Click Dispatch for Delivery as described above

Shipday Settings in Newclear​

SettingDescription
Default Delivery WindowStandard hours block for scheduled deliveries
Auto-dispatch on paymentAutomatically send to Shipday when order is marked Paid
Customer notificationSend tracking link to customer email/SMS automatically
Webhook URLNewclear's webhook endpoint for Shipday status updates

Troubleshooting​

Dispatch failed — "Address not found"

  • Verify the customer's delivery address is complete and correctly formatted
  • Shipday geocodes addresses; ensure the street, city, and ZIP are present

Driver not receiving notifications

  • Check driver is active and online in the Shipday driver app
  • Verify driver's phone notifications are enabled

Delivery status not updating in Newclear

  • Check that the Shipday webhook is configured correctly in Shipday settings
  • Verify Newclear's webhook URL is entered in your Shipday account
