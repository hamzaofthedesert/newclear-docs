# Newclear Mobile App

The **Newclear Mobile App** is a native mobile application built with Flutter, bringing the power of Newclear to your iOS and Android devices. Manage inventory, process sales, scan products, and monitor your business from anywhere.

---

## What the Mobile App Does

The Newclear mobile app is designed for:

- **On-the-go inventory management** — Adjust stock, scan barcodes, check product details
- **Mobile POS** — Process sales from a tablet on the sales floor
- **Order monitoring** — View and update order status from your phone
- **Dashboard at a glance** — Key metrics wherever you are
- **Barcode scanning** — Use your phone's camera as a barcode scanner
- **Push notifications** — Real-time alerts for orders, low stock, and more

## How to Download the APK (Android)

The Newclear mobile app is distributed as a direct APK download:

1. On your Android device, open a browser and go to your Newclear instance or ask your admin for the APK download link
2. Download the APK file
3. You may need to enable **"Install from Unknown Sources"** in your Android settings:
   - Go to **Settings → Security → Install Unknown Apps**
   - Enable for your browser app
4. Open the downloaded APK file
5. Tap **Install**
6. The Newclear app is installed and appears on your home screen

:::note
The APK is distributed by your Newclear administrator, not through the Google Play Store. This is standard for enterprise B2B apps. The APK is signed and safe to install.
:::

## iOS Download

For iPhone and iPad, download from the **App Store**:

1. Open the App Store
2. Search for **Newclear**
3. Tap **Get** to install

Or visit the link provided by your Newclear administrator.

## Connecting to Your Newclear Account (Newclear Connect)

When you first open the app, you'll need to connect it to your Newclear account:

### Option A: QR Code (Recommended)

1. Open the Newclear mobile app
2. Tap **Scan QR to Connect**
3. On your Newclear web account, go to **Settings → Connect**
4. A QR code is displayed on screen
5. Scan it with the mobile app
6. The app is connected automatically — no typing needed

### Option B: Manual Entry

1. In the app, tap **Manual Setup**
2. Enter your Newclear server URL (e.g., `https://prod.newclear.io`)
3. Enter your email and password
4. Tap **Connect**

:::tip
Use the QR code method — it's faster and eliminates typos.
:::

## Key Features on Mobile

### 📦 Product Management
- Browse and search your product catalog
- View product details, images, stock levels
- Quick stock adjustments with the physical barcode scanner or camera scan

### 🛒 Mobile POS
- Full POS functionality on tablet
- Barcode scanning via device camera
- Card payments (pair a Stripe reader to the mobile POS)
- Customer lookup and creation

### 📊 Dashboard
- Key metrics optimized for small screen
- Sales charts, pending orders, reorder alerts

### 🏷️ Barcode Scanner
- Use your phone camera or a paired Bluetooth scanner
- Scan any product to instantly view its details
- Scan to perform stock adjustments on the spot

### 📋 Orders
- View all orders from all channels
- Update fulfillment status on mobile

### 🔔 Push Notifications
- Receive real-time alerts for new orders, low stock, and more
- Configure notification preferences in the app settings

## Keeping the App Updated

When a new version of the Newclear app is available:
