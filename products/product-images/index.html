Product Images

Product images are stored in Newclear and pushed to your connected sales channels. Good images directly impact conversion rates — on eBay and Amazon especially, listings with multiple clear photos significantly outperform those with one or none.

Accessing the Images Tab​

  1. Go to Products and click on any product to open it
  2. Click the Images tab at the top of the edit page

Uploading Images​

  1. Click Upload Image or drag and drop files into the upload area
  2. Select one or more image files from your computer
  3. Images are uploaded and processed immediately

Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP
Recommended size: At least 800×800px. For eBay, 1600×1600px or larger is ideal for zoom functionality
File size: Keep under 5MB per image for best performance

tip

Use a white or neutral background for product shots on marketplace channels (eBay, Amazon, Walmart). Lifestyle or contextual photos can be added as secondary images.

Setting the Thumbnail / Primary Image​

The first image in the list is always the primary/thumbnail — it's what shows in your product list, on channel listings, and on POS receipts.

To change the primary image:

  • Drag and drop the image you want first to the top position
  • Or click a reorder handle (≡) and drag it to position 1

Reordering Images​

All images can be reordered by dragging. The order in Newclear is the order they'll appear on your channel listings (eBay, Shopify, etc.). Consider putting:

  1. Main product photo (front, clean background)
  2. Back or side view
  3. Detail shots
  4. Box/packaging (if applicable)
  5. Lifestyle/in-use photo

Deleting an Image​

Click the X or Delete icon on any image thumbnail to remove it. This also removes it from any connected channel listings on the next sync.

warning

Deleting the primary image when a product has an active eBay listing may temporarily remove the main photo until the next sync pushes an updated image. Avoid deleting primary images for active high-traffic listings.

Image Sources​

Images can come from multiple sources in Newclear:

SourceHow
Manual uploadUpload from your computer on the Images tab
Mobile appTake photos in the AI Product Builder flow on your phone
AI enrichmentAI may suggest image URLs for well-known products
URL importPaste an image URL during product creation or CSV import
CSV importInclude an image_url column in your import file

S3 Storage​

All uploaded images are stored securely in Amazon S3 cloud storage. This means:

  • Images are always accessible from any device
  • They don't take up space on your computer
  • Deleted images are removed from storage (cannot be recovered)
  • Fast global CDN delivery to your channel listings

Tips for Best Results​

tip

Batch photos before importing. If you're doing a bulk import via CSV, pre-photograph your products and include an image_url column pointing to publicly accessible image URLs. This saves a lot of time vs. uploading individually afterward.

tip

Mobile photos work great. The Newclear mobile app lets you take photos directly from your phone during the AI Product Builder flow. For small/mid-volume sellers, this is the fastest way to get product photos attached.

note

Images pushed to eBay must meet eBay's image policies — at least 500×500px, no watermarks on the first image, no borders. Newclear doesn't enforce these in the UI, so ensure your photos comply.
