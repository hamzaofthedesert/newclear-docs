Sellers

Sellers are sub-accounts within your Newclear company used to organize products by brand, location, business line, or any logical grouping that makes sense for your operation. Think of a Seller as a bucket that products belong to.

What Sellers Are​

A Seller in Newclear is not a person — it's an organizational unit. Examples of how businesses use Sellers:

  • Multi-brand businesses: "Brand A" and "Brand B" as separate sellers so you can filter/report independently
  • Multi-location retail: "Store - Main St" and "Store - Mall Location" as separate sellers
  • Channel separation: "eBay Store" vs "Amazon Store" vs "Wholesale"
  • Liquidation tracking: "Lot #42 - Target Return" as a seller to track that lot's P&L

Every product in Newclear must be assigned to a Seller. If you're a small single-brand business, you'll typically have just one Seller — but you still need to create it.

Creating a Seller​

  1. Go to Settings > Sellers
  2. Click New Seller
  3. Fill in:
    • Name — The seller/brand name (e.g., "Main Store", "ABC Liquidations")
    • Description — Optional notes about what this seller represents
  4. Click Save

Assigning Products to a Seller​

When creating a product, you select the Seller in the Seller dropdown field. Every product belongs to exactly one Seller.

To change a product's seller:

  1. Open the product in edit mode
  2. Change the Seller dropdown
  3. Save

To reassign many products at once, use the Import Wizard with the seller column mapped.

Filtering by Seller​

The Seller filter appears throughout Newclear:

  • Products page — Filter to show only one seller's products
  • Inventory / Stock view — Filter by seller
  • Sales History — Filter reports by seller to see revenue per brand/location
  • POS — Select seller before checkout (determines which seller's products appear in search)

Seller Picker in POW​

When using the POW warehouse receiving system, you must select a Seller before receiving a transfer. Products received will be assigned to that Seller.

This is important: if your company receives stock from another Newclear company, you need to specify which of your internal sellers will "own" the incoming inventory.

👉 See Selecting Your Seller in POW

Channel Connections Per Seller​

Sales channels (eBay, Shopify, etc.) are connected at the company level, not per seller. However, when a product is pushed to a channel, its Seller assignment is preserved in Newclear so you can filter channel listings by seller.

Tips​

tip

Start simple. If you're a single-brand business, create one Seller named after your brand or store. You can always add more later as your business grows.

tip

If you're running a liquidation operation where you receive different pallets/lots, consider making each lot a separate Seller temporarily. This makes P&L tracking much easier — just filter by that Seller in Sales History to see how a specific lot performed.

note

Sellers are different from Users/Employees. See Inviting Your Team for information about adding staff accounts.

warning

You cannot delete a Seller that has products assigned to it. Reassign or delete the products first, or archive the seller instead.
