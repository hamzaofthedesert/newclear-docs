Stock Levels & Inventory

Newclear tracks stock quantities in real time across all your sales channels. When a sale happens anywhere — POS, eBay, Shopify, or any connected channel — inventory is decremented automatically. When stock hits zero, listings are paused to prevent overselling.

How Quantity Is Tracked​

Every product has a quantity on hand (qty) field. This is the single source of truth. Newclear is the master for inventory — channels reflect what Newclear says, not the other way around.

When a sale occurs:

  • POS sale: Qty decremented immediately at checkout
  • Channel order pulled: Qty decremented when the order is imported
  • Manual adjustment: You update qty directly in the product or inventory view

Viewing Inventory​

Go to Inventory (or Products) in the left sidebar. You'll see a list of all products with their current quantities. Use filters to narrow down:

  • Filter by Seller to see one brand's stock
  • Filter by Status to see active vs draft
  • Search for a specific product

For auto parts, use the Vehicle Filter to find parts that fit a specific year/make/model. See Vehicle Filter.

Updating Stock Manually​

To adjust quantity for a single product:

  1. Open the product (click its name)
  2. Find the Quantity field on the Details tab
  3. Change the number
  4. Click Save
tip

If you're doing a physical inventory count, update quantities in Newclear as you count. The changes push to channels automatically so your listings stay accurate.

Bin Locations​

Newclear supports bin locations — a short text field indicating where in your warehouse the product is physically stored (e.g., "Shelf A3", "Bin 12", "Back Room - Left").

To set a bin location:

  1. Open the product
  2. Find the Bin Location field
  3. Enter the location code
  4. Save

Bin locations appear on the inventory list and can be filtered/sorted, making it easy to direct staff to the right shelf during order picking.

What Happens When Stock Hits Zero​

When a product's quantity reaches 0:

  • eBay: The listing is automatically ended (or quantity set to 0, which eBay treats as inactive)
  • Shopify: The product is marked as out of stock (continues to show with "Out of Stock" if your theme supports it, or hidden depending on settings)
  • Amazon: Inventory quantity sent as 0; listing goes inactive
  • WooCommerce: Stock set to 0; product shows as out of stock
  • POS: A warning appears when scanning an out-of-stock item. Staff can override if needed (see Out-of-Stock Handling)

When you add stock back:

  • Channels are updated automatically on the next sync
  • Paused listings go live again
warning

Don't set quantity to a large fake number (like 9999) to keep listings live. This leads to overselling — you'll sell items you don't have. Always keep quantities accurate.

Inventory Adjustments for Receiving​

When you receive a warehouse transfer via POW, product quantities are incremented automatically. See Receiving a Transfer.

When you import products via CSV, you can include a quantity column to set initial stock. See Import Wizard.

Low Stock Awareness​

Newclear can notify you when stock falls below a threshold. Set this up in your Notifications Settings.

Tips​

tip

Do a regular (weekly or monthly) physical inventory reconciliation, especially for high-volume or high-value items. Compare your Newclear quantities against physical counts and update accordingly.

note

Newclear tracks quantity as a whole number. Fractional quantities (e.g., for products sold by weight) are not currently supported.
