Label Sizes & Customization

Newclear supports four label sizes to match common thermal label stock and general printing formats. You choose the size each time you print, and your last selection is remembered in your browser.

Available Label Sizes​

SizeDimensionsBest For
2×22 inches × 2 inchesSmall items, jewelry, phone accessories
2×32 inches × 3 inchesStandard retail items, clothing tags
3×23 inches × 2 inchesWide format, fits more title text
4×24 inches × 2 inchesThermal shipping-style labels, auto parts

Choosing the Right Size​

2×2 (Small Square)​

Best for small or lightweight items where a large label would be awkward:

  • Jewelry, accessories
  • Phone cases and cables
  • Small toy or collectible packaging

2×3 (Standard Portrait)​

The most versatile size for general retail:

  • Clothing tags
  • Shoes boxes
  • Electronics accessories
  • Books and media

3×2 (Wide Landscape)​

Gives more horizontal space — good when titles are long:

  • Clothing with long descriptions
  • Multi-word brand/model names
  • Anything where you need a wider barcode for reliable scanning

4×2 (Long Label)​

Matches standard thermal label rolls (like Zebra 4×2"):

  • Auto parts (long SKUs and model numbers)
  • Appliance parts
  • Industrial components
  • Any use with a standard 4×2" thermal printer

Selecting a Size​

When you open the print dialog:

  1. Click the Size selector
  2. Choose from the four options
  3. A preview updates immediately to show how the label will look at that size
  4. Click Print

Size Preference Saved in Browser​

Your last selected label size is saved in your browser's localStorage (not in your Newclear account). This means:

  • Your size preference is remembered across sessions on the same browser
  • If you switch computers or browsers, you'll need to re-select
  • It's per-device, not per-account — different team members can have different default sizes on their own computers
note

Because size preferences are stored in the browser (not the server), clearing browser data or switching to a private/incognito window resets the size preference.

Matching Your Label Stock​

If you use a thermal label printer (via Newclear Connect), make sure your label size in Newclear matches the physical label roll loaded in the printer:

  • Zebra GK420d with 4×2" labels → Select 4×2 in Newclear
  • Dymo 450 with 2.25×1.25" → Select the nearest size (2×2 or 2×3)

Mismatched sizes cause labels to print incorrectly (cut off, overlapping, or too much blank space).

Customization Limits​

The current label sizes are fixed — there's no custom dimension input. Field layout (which data appears on Price vs SKU labels) is also fixed.

If you need a custom label format (custom size, custom fields, logo on label), contact Newclear support — custom label templates may be available for higher-tier plans.

Tips​

tip

Test print on plain paper before loading expensive thermal label stock. This lets you verify the size looks right and the barcode scans correctly before wasting label material.

tip

For thermal printers: if your barcodes are coming out too small to scan reliably, switch to a larger label size (e.g., from 2×2 to 4×2). Larger barcodes scan faster and from greater distances.
