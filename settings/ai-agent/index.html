Newclear AI Agent

The Newclear AI Agent is a conversational AI assistant that has knowledge of your specific company's inventory, sales, and channels. Ask it questions in plain English and get answers based on your actual business data.

What the AI Agent Is​

Unlike a general-purpose AI (which knows about the world but not your business), the Newclear AI Agent is connected to your Newclear data. It can:

  • Answer questions about your inventory — what you have, how much, where it is
  • Analyze your sales — what's selling, what's not, revenue trends
  • Summarize channel performance — which channels are performing best
  • Help with business decisions — based on your actual data, not generic advice

Requirements​

warning

The AI Agent requires an OpenAI API key configured in your company settings.

Go to Settings > API Keys to add your key.

Accessing the AI Agent​

  1. Look for the AI or Chat icon in the sidebar or top navigation
  2. Click to open the AI Agent chat panel
  3. Type your question and press Enter

What You Can Ask (Tier 1 Capabilities)​

The AI Agent's current capabilities focus on inventory and sales intelligence:

Inventory Questions​

  • "How many products do I have in stock?"
  • "What are my 10 lowest stock items?"
  • "Show me everything in the Electronics category"
  • "What products haven't sold in the last 30 days?"
  • "How many items do I have tagged as HOLD?"

Sales Questions​

  • "What was my total revenue this month?"
  • "What are my top 5 best-selling products?"
  • "How much profit did I make this week?"
  • "Compare my eBay sales vs POS sales for October"
  • "Which seller had the highest revenue last month?"

Channel Questions​

  • "How many eBay listings do I have?"
  • "Which products have listing errors on eBay?"
  • "What channels are connected to my account?"

Business Summaries​

  • "Give me a summary of this week's performance"
  • "How is my liquidation business doing?"
  • "What's my average order value?"

What the AI Can't Do (Yet)​

The AI Agent currently reads your data — it cannot:

  • Create or edit products
  • Trigger channel syncs
  • Process payments
  • Send emails on your behalf
  • Access external websites or real-time market data

These capabilities are on the roadmap for future tiers of the AI Agent.

How Responses Work​

The AI Agent queries your Newclear database in real-time to answer your question. Responses include:

  • Direct answers with numbers
  • Brief analysis or context
  • Suggestions for what to look at next

For complex questions, the AI may provide a summary with a link to the relevant Newclear report.

Privacy and Data​

The AI Agent uses your OpenAI API key. Your business data (inventory, sales, etc.) is included in the context of the query sent to OpenAI. OpenAI's standard data privacy policies apply. Newclear does not share your data beyond what's needed to answer your question.

Tips​

tip

Be specific in your questions. "How are sales?" is vague. "What's my gross profit for this month compared to last month?" gets you a clear, useful answer.

tip

Use the AI Agent for your morning briefing: "Give me a summary of yesterday's sales and any issues I should know about." This 30-second check replaces manually pulling several reports.

note

The AI Agent is a newer feature and improves continuously. If it can't answer a specific question, try rephrasing or break it into smaller questions.
