Printing from Web & Mobile

Once Newclear Connect is installed and paired, printing labels from both the web app and the mobile app is simple. This guide covers how to trigger print jobs and what to do if something goes wrong.

Printing from the Web App​

Selecting Products to Print​

  1. Go to Products or Inventory
  2. Select one or more products using the checkboxes
  3. Click Print Labels (printer icon or button)

The Print Dialog​

A dialog appears with:

  • Label Type — Price Label or SKU Label
  • Label Size — 2×2, 2×3, 3×2, or 4×2
  • Printer — Select from available printers connected via Newclear Connect (or "Browser" for standard printing)
  • Preview — See how the label will look before printing
  1. Configure your selections
  2. Click Print

If you selected a ZPL printer via Connect, the job is sent directly. If you selected "Browser", your browser's print dialog opens.

Printing from the Mobile App​

Print Bottom Sheet​

From any product detail page on mobile:

  1. Tap the Print or Label icon
  2. A bottom sheet slides up with options:
    • Label type (Price / SKU)
    • Label size
    • Printer selection (lists printers available via Connect on your network)
  3. Tap Print

How Mobile Printing Works​

Mobile printing requires Newclear Connect running on a computer on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone:

  1. Mobile app sends print job to Newclear's servers
  2. Newclear routes the job to your company's Newclear Connect instance
  3. Connect receives the job and sends it to the printer

For this to work:

  • Newclear Connect must be running and signed in
  • Your phone and the Connect computer must be on the same network (or both have internet access — Connect uses the Newclear cloud as relay)
  • The printer must be connected to the Connect computer

How Print Jobs Flow Through Connect​

Web/Mobile → Newclear Cloud → Newclear Connect → Printer

Connect polls Newclear's servers for pending print jobs every few seconds. When a job arrives:

  1. Connect generates the ZPL label data from the product info
  2. Sends the ZPL commands to the selected printer
  3. Reports job completion back to Newclear
  4. Web/Mobile receives a confirmation

Troubleshooting​

"Printer offline" or "No printers available"​

  • Check that Newclear Connect is running (look for the tray icon)
  • Check that the printer is powered on and connected (USB or network)
  • In Connect settings, verify the printer is added and shows as online
  • Try restarting Newclear Connect

Labels not printing but no error shown​

  • Open Newclear Connect and check the print queue for stuck jobs
  • Clear the queue and try printing again
  • Check the printer for error lights or paper jam

"Connect not paired"​

  • Newclear Connect may have been signed out
  • Open Connect and sign in again with your Newclear credentials

Print job sent but blank labels come out​

  • The label size in Newclear doesn't match the physical label stock
  • Adjust the size selection to match your label roll
tip

After printing, always do a quick test scan of the printed barcode. This confirms the barcode is readable before labeling your entire inventory.
