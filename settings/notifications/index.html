Notifications & Alerts

Newclear keeps you informed in real time with in-app notifications and optional email alerts. The notification system is powered by Pusher for instant delivery — when an order arrives, you see it immediately without refreshing the page.

The Notification Bell​

In the top-right corner of Newclear, there's a bell icon (🔔). This is your notification center.

  • A red badge appears when you have unread notifications (e.g., 🔔 3)
  • Click the bell to open the notification dropdown
  • Recent notifications are listed with timestamp and description
  • Click a notification to navigate to the relevant item

Notification Types​

🛒 New Order​

Trigger: A new order arrives from any channel (eBay, Shopify, Amazon, WooCommerce, Walmart)
Message: "New order #12345 from eBay — $87.50"
Action: Click to open the order

Real-time via Pusher for webhook-based channels (Shopify). Near real-time for polling channels (eBay — may be 15–30 min after the order was placed).

⚠️ Low Stock​

Trigger: A product's quantity drops below your configured threshold
Message: "Low stock: [Product Name] — Qty: 2"
Action: Click to open the product and update inventory

Low stock threshold can be configured per-product or globally in settings.

❌ Sync Errors​

Trigger: A channel listing encounters an error (invalid category, missing field, rejected by eBay, etc.)
Message: "eBay listing error: [Product Name] — 'Item condition not available for this category'"
Action: Click to open the product's Channels tab and see the error detail

tip

Enable sync error notifications and act on them promptly. An eBay listing error means that product isn't selling on eBay until it's fixed. A few minutes to fix the error can recover a sale you'd otherwise miss.

📊 Report Ready​

Trigger: An async export or report job has completed (e.g., a large CSV export you initiated)
Message: "Your Sales History export is ready — click to download"
Action: Click to get the download link

💬 System Messages​

Trigger: Newclear platform announcements, maintenance notices, or account-level alerts (payment failure, trial ending, etc.)

Marking Notifications as Read​

  • Click a notification to mark it as read (and navigate to the item)
  • Click Mark All as Read in the notification dropdown to clear the badge
  • Notifications older than 30 days are automatically removed

Email Alerts​

For important notifications, Newclear can also send emails:

  1. Go to Settings > Notifications
  2. Toggle email alerts on for each notification type:
    • New orders: email for every order / daily digest / weekly digest / off
    • Sync errors: email immediately / off
    • Reports ready: email immediately / off
    • Low stock: email daily digest / off
tip

For order alerts: if you check Newclear regularly throughout the day, in-app notifications are sufficient. If you're frequently away from the computer, turn on email alerts for new orders so you never miss one.

Real-Time vs Delayed​

Notification TypeDelivery
New Shopify orderReal-time (seconds)
New eBay/Amazon order~15–30 min after order placed
Sync errorNear real-time
Low stockWithin minutes of stock change
Report readyWithin seconds of completion

Tips​

note

Notifications are per-user — your notification settings don't affect what your team members see. Each employee configures their own notification preferences.

tip

If you're a manager or admin, turn on Sync Error notifications. These are the most actionable — every error is a potential missed sale that you can often fix in under a minute.
