Employee Management

Newclear includes employee management features for adding team members, assigning roles, managing their access, and tracking work history. This is separate from the Sellers system — employees are people who log in and use Newclear.

Accessing Employee Management​

  1. Go to Settings
  2. Click Users or Employees

Adding an Employee​

  1. Click Add Employee or New User
  2. Fill in:
    • First Name, Last Name
    • Email — Used as their login username
    • Password — Set an initial password (they can change it later)
    • Role — Admin, Manager, or Staff
    • Hourly Rate — Dollar amount per hour (optional, used for payroll)
    • Phone — Optional contact number
  3. Click Save

The employee can now log in at newclear.systems with their email and password.

Role Assignments​

Roles control what each employee can see and do:

Admin​

Full access to everything including settings, billing, and channel management. Assign to owners and senior managers.

Manager​

Full operational access — products, orders, POS, POW, imports, reports. Cannot change billing, remove users, or manage channel OAuth connections.

Staff​

Limited to day-to-day operations — POS, POW receiving, inventory browsing, mobile app. Cannot edit product details, view profit reports, or access Settings.

👉 See Inviting Your Team for the full role comparison table.

Editing an Employee Profile​

  1. Click on the employee's name in the list
  2. Update any fields (name, email, phone, hourly rate, role)
  3. Click Save

Role changes take effect immediately on the employee's next page load.

Changing an Employee's Password​

  1. Open the employee's profile
  2. Click Reset Password or enter a new password in the password field
  3. Save

The employee should log out and log back in with the new password. Alternatively, they can use "Forgot Password" on the login page.

Deactivating an Employee​

When a team member leaves:

  1. Open their profile
  2. Toggle Active to OFF (or click Deactivate)
  3. Save

Deactivated employees:

  • Cannot log in
  • Their history (sales, POW receives, attendance) is preserved
  • They appear in the employee list with a "Deactivated" status
  • Can be reactivated at any time
warning

Deactivate rather than delete. Deleting an employee permanently removes their record and any history associated with them (attendance records, etc.).

Employee vs Seller — Key Difference​

EmployeeSeller
Has a login?✅ Yes❌ No
Is a person?✅ Yes❌ No (it's an org/brand)
PurposeAccess control and trackingProduct organization
ExampleSarah (warehouse manager)"Brand A" or "Store Location B"

Employees use the system. Sellers are what products belong to. See Sellers for more.

Tips​

tip

When you hire a new employee, create their Newclear account on their first day so they can start using the system immediately. Give them Staff role initially — you can always promote to Manager once they've demonstrated they know the system.

tip

Do a quarterly review of your employee list to deactivate anyone who's no longer with you. Former employees shouldn't have login access to your business data.
