Exporting Data

Newclear lets you export your sales history and product data to CSV files for external analysis, accounting, backup, or sharing with your team or accountant.

Exporting Sales to CSV​

From the Sales History page:

  1. Go to Reports > Sales History
  2. Apply your desired filters (date range, seller, channel, etc.)
  3. Click Export or Download CSV
  4. Newclear starts preparing the export

Async Export Process​

For large exports (thousands of orders), the export runs asynchronously in the background:

  1. You click Export
  2. Newclear shows a "Generating report..." message or queues the job
  3. You'll receive a notification (bell icon) when the export is ready
  4. Click the notification or a Download link to get the file

Small exports (a few hundred rows) may generate instantly and download directly.

tip

Don't click Export multiple times if it seems slow. Each click creates a separate export job. Wait for the notification that your file is ready.

What's Included in the Sales CSV​

The export includes a row per order line item (one row per product sold) with columns including:

Exporting Product Catalog​

From the Products page:

  1. Click Export (or go to Products > Export)
  2. Apply any filters first if you want a subset (e.g., one seller)
  3. Click Export — generates a CSV with your product catalog

Product exports include all product fields: title, SKU, vendor_sku, price, cost, MSRP, condition, category, qty, images, tags, etc.

Using Exported Data​

For accounting: Import the sales CSV into your accounting software (QuickBooks, Wave, Xero) or share with your bookkeeper.

For analysis: Open in Excel or Google Sheets. Use pivot tables to analyze sales by channel, seller, or category.

For backup: Regular exports serve as a data backup of your sales records.

For tax preparation: Filter by year and export all sales for annual tax reporting.

Scheduled Exports​

Newclear doesn't currently offer automated scheduled exports. Exports are on-demand. To get recurring exports, set a calendar reminder to run them on your desired frequency (weekly, monthly).

Tips​

tip

Export at the end of each month as a routine. Even if you don't analyze it immediately, having a local CSV backup of your sales data is good practice.

note

Exported CSV files are in UTF-8 encoding. If you see garbled characters when opening in Excel on Windows, open Excel first, then import the CSV via Data > From Text/CSV and select UTF-8 encoding.
