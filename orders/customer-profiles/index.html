Customer Profiles

Newclear maintains a customer directory that keeps track of who buys from you — both in-store and online. Customer profiles let you look up purchase history, associate sales with specific people, and manage B2B relationships.

The Customer List​

Go to Customers in the sidebar to see your full customer directory. The list shows:

  • Name
  • Email
  • Phone
  • Total purchases
  • Last purchase date
  • B2B flag (if marked as a wholesale customer)

Use the search bar to find customers by name, email, or phone.

Creating a Customer Profile​

Automatically (From Channel Orders)​

When a marketplace order (eBay, Amazon, Shopify) is imported, Newclear automatically creates or updates a customer profile using the buyer's shipping info.

Manually (For POS / Walk-In Customers)​

  1. Go to Customers in the sidebar
  2. Click New Customer
  3. Fill in:
    • First Name, Last Name — Required
    • Email — Recommended; used for receipts and communication
    • Phone — Useful for layaway and contact
    • Company Name — For B2B customers
    • Shipping Address — Default shipping address
    • Notes — Any internal notes about this customer
    • B2B — Check this box for wholesale/business customers (see below)
  4. Click Save

Customer Fields Explained​

FieldUse
NameDisplayed on orders, receipts, and reports
EmailReceipt delivery, order notifications
PhoneContact for layaway, hold items, delivery
CompanyB2B customers; appears on wholesale invoices
AddressPre-fills shipping on channel orders
NotesInternal staff notes (not visible to customer)
B2B flagMarks customer as a business buyer — may unlock wholesale pricing

Purchase History​

Open any customer profile to see their full purchase history:

  • List of all orders (POS and channel) associated with this customer
  • Date, order total, payment method, channel for each
  • Link to each order for full details

This is useful for:

  • Confirming a past purchase for a return
  • Seeing how frequently a customer buys and how much they spend
  • Identifying your top customers

Assigning a Customer to a POS Sale​

During POS checkout, use the Customer search field:

  1. Type the customer's name or email
  2. Select from the dropdown results
  3. The customer is attached to the sale

Even if you don't look up a customer (anonymous walk-in), the sale is still recorded — just without a customer attached.

B2B Flag​

Customers marked as B2B (business-to-business):

  • Are flagged visually in the customer list
  • May have access to wholesale pricing (if configured)
  • Play a role in the B2B Wholesale feature

To mark a customer as B2B:

  1. Open their profile
  2. Toggle the B2B checkbox
  3. Save

Merging Duplicate Customers​

If a customer profile was created multiple times (e.g., once from eBay, once manually), contact support to merge duplicate profiles. Order history from both profiles is preserved.

Tips​

tip

Always look up a customer before creating a new one. Duplicate profiles clutter your directory and split purchase history. Use the search before clicking "New Customer."

tip

For your top regular customers, add their profile before they arrive so you can look them up quickly at POS. It's a nice personal touch to greet them by name and reference their history.
