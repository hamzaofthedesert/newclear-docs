Vehicle Filter

The Vehicle Filter lets you browse your inventory and find all parts compatible with a specific vehicle. It's a cascade selector — choose the Year, then Make, then Model — and your product list narrows to show only matching parts.

Where the Vehicle Filter Appears​

The Vehicle Filter is available on:

  • Products page — Browse and manage compatible parts
  • Inventory page — View stock levels for parts that fit a specific vehicle

It appears as a filter panel (alongside other filters like Seller, Category, etc.) when your company has products in Auto Parts categories.

Using the Vehicle Filter​

Step 1: Select Year​

Click the Year dropdown and select the model year you're searching for. Available years are derived from your existing fitment data — only years present in your catalog are shown.

Step 2: Select Make​

After selecting a year, the Make dropdown populates with only the manufacturers that have fitment data for that year in your catalog. Select the manufacturer.

Step 3: Select Model​

After selecting a make, the Model dropdown populates with only the models that fit the selected year and make. Select the model.

The product list immediately updates to show all parts with a matching fitment row.

Cascade Behavior​

The filter is cascading — each level narrows the options available in the next:

  • Year selected → Only makes with fitment for that year appear
  • Year + Make selected → Only models for that year/make appear

This prevents impossible combinations (e.g., "1985 Tesla") from appearing.

What the Filter Shows​

The vehicle filter shows products in your catalog that have fitment data matching the selected vehicle. This is not a comprehensive vehicle database — it only shows what you've added fitment rows for.

If you searched for "2019 Ford Mustang" and found nothing, it means none of your current products have a fitment row for that vehicle.

Clearing the Vehicle Filter​

To remove the vehicle filter:

  1. Click Clear Vehicle or the X next to the vehicle filter
  2. The product list returns to showing all products (with any other active filters still applied)

You can also change the vehicle selection by picking a different Year (which resets Make and Model selections).

Combining with Other Filters​

The vehicle filter works alongside all other filters:

  • Vehicle filter + Seller = "All parts for a 2019 Ford F-150 from my 'Main Store' seller"
  • Vehicle filter + Status = "All Active parts for a 2018 Honda Civic"
  • Vehicle filter + Category = "All brake parts for a 2020 Toyota Camry"

Use Cases for Vehicle Filter​

Staff assisting customers: Customer walks in asking for parts for their 2017 Chevy Silverado. Staff selects Year=2017, Make=Chevrolet, Model=Silverado — instantly sees all compatible parts in stock.

Inventory review: Check what you have in stock for popular vehicles to identify gaps in your catalog.

Pricing review: Filter by vehicle to see all parts for a specific model and review their pricing together.

Tips​

tip

The vehicle filter is only as good as your fitment data. Invest time in adding accurate fitment rows to your products (manually or via AI enrichment) — the vehicle filter becomes a powerful customer service and inventory tool as a result.

note

The vehicle filter doesn't appear on the Products page if you have no products in Auto Parts categories. Once you create an Auto Parts category and add fitment to any product, the filter becomes visible.
