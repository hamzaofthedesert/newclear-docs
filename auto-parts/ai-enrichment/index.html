AI Fitment Enrichment

AI Fitment Enrichment uses OpenAI to automatically generate vehicle compatibility rows for your auto parts products. Instead of manually researching and entering fitment data, the AI reads your product title and description to determine which vehicles the part fits.

What It Does​

When you click ✨ AI Enrich on a product's Fitment tab:

  1. Newclear sends the product's title, description, and any existing fitment data to OpenAI
  2. OpenAI analyzes the text for vehicle compatibility clues (make/model mentions, year ranges, part numbers)
  3. The AI returns a structured list of compatible vehicles
  4. Newclear creates fitment rows for each vehicle

Year Range Expansion​

If the product description says "fits 2015-2019 Toyota Camry," the AI expands the range into individual rows:

  • 2015 Toyota Camry
  • 2016 Toyota Camry
  • 2017 Toyota Camry
  • 2018 Toyota Camry
  • 2019 Toyota Camry

Each row is stored individually so eBay can properly display and filter all compatible model years.

Requirements​

warning

AI Fitment Enrichment requires an OpenAI API key configured in your company settings.

Go to Settings > API Keys to add your key.

Using AI Fitment Enrichment​

  1. Open a product in an Auto Parts category
  2. Click the Fitment tab
  3. Click ✨ Enrich or ✨ AI Fitment
  4. Wait 10–30 seconds while the AI processes
  5. New fitment rows appear in the table
  6. Review the rows for accuracy

The Guardrail: AI Never Guesses​

A key design principle of AI Fitment Enrichment:

If the AI can't determine fitment from the title/description, it returns nothing rather than guessing.

This prevents bad fitment data from entering your listings. An empty fitment table is better than incorrect fitment that causes customer returns.

note

If AI Enrichment generates no rows, it means the title/description doesn't contain enough vehicle-specific information. Add more details to the description (e.g., which vehicles it fits, the OEM part number) and run it again.

What Information Helps the AI​

The AI performs better with more specific product information:

InformationExample
Vehicle mentions in title"2015-2020 Ford F-150 Front Bumper"
OEM part numbers"Replaces OEM# 1234567890"
Engine specs in description"Compatible with 3.5L EcoBoost and 5.0L V8 engines"
Trim-specific notes"Fits XLT, Lariat, and Platinum trims"
Model year ranges"Fits model years 2018-2022"

A vague title like "Car Mirror" gives the AI nothing to work with. A title like "2016-2021 Honda Civic Driver Side Power Mirror Black" gives the AI everything it needs.

Reviewing AI-Generated Fitment​

After enrichment, always review the generated rows:

  • Look for obviously wrong vehicles (wrong make/model combinations)
  • Check that the year range is correct
  • Verify trim/engine specifics are accurate
  • Delete any rows that are clearly incorrect
tip

Start with AI enrichment as a foundation, then manually edit/add/remove specific rows. This hybrid approach is much faster than fully manual entry.

Bulk AI Fitment for Large Catalogs​

For businesses with hundreds of auto parts, you can run AI fitment enrichment on multiple products at once:

  1. Select multiple products from the Products list (use checkboxes)
  2. Click Bulk Actions > AI Enrich Fitment
  3. The system queues enrichment jobs for each product
  4. Monitor progress via notifications

This is how businesses with large catalogs process fitment efficiently. See Bulk Import with Fitment for a real-world example.
