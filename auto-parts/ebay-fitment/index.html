Fitment & eBay Listings

When an auto parts product with fitment data is listed on eBay, Newclear automatically injects the vehicle compatibility list into the eBay listing XML. This makes your parts discoverable by buyers searching for parts for their specific vehicle.

How Fitment Flows to eBay​

When Newclear pushes an auto parts product to eBay:

  1. Newclear reads all fitment rows from the product's Fitment tab
  2. Each row (year/make/model/trim/engine) is converted to an eBay ItemCompatibility entry
  3. All entries are packaged into an ItemCompatibilityList block in the eBay XML
  4. eBay receives the complete listing data including the compatibility list

This all happens automatically — you don't need to do anything extra beyond having fitment data on the product.

What Buyers See on eBay​

When a listing has fitment data:

  • "Fits your vehicle" badge — Appears when a buyer searches from their My Garage or searches by vehicle
  • Parts Compatibility tab — On the listing page, buyers can click to see the full list of compatible vehicles
  • Vehicle search filter — Buyers browsing eBay Motors can filter by vehicle and find your part

These features significantly improve visibility and buyer confidence.

eBay's ItemCompatibilityList Format​

For technical reference, here's what Newclear sends to eBay for each fitment row:

<ItemCompatibility>
  <CompatibilityNotes>notes if any</CompatibilityNotes>
  <NameValueList>
    <Name>Year</Name><Value>2018</Value>
  </NameValueList>
  <NameValueList>
    <Name>Make</Name><Value>Ford</Value>
  </NameValueList>
  <NameValueList>
    <Name>Model</Name><Value>F-150</Value>
  </NameValueList>
  <NameValueList>
    <Name>Trim</Name><Value>XLT</Value>
  </NameValueList>
  <NameValueList>
    <Name>Engine</Name><Value>5.0L V8</Value>
  </NameValueList>
</ItemCompatibility>

eBay has a maximum of 3,000 compatibility entries per listing.

Conditions for Fitment Injection​

Fitment is only included in eBay listings when:

  • ✅ The product has at least one fitment row
  • ✅ The product is in an Auto Parts category
  • ✅ The category is mapped to a valid eBay Motors category (Parts & Accessories or similar)

If any condition isn't met, the listing is created without fitment data.

Existing Listings​

Important: If a product already has an active eBay listing and you add fitment for the first time (or update existing fitment):

  • The fitment is included in the next update sent to eBay
  • eBay updates the existing listing with the new compatibility data
  • The listing does NOT need to be ended and re-listed — updates go to the existing listing

Similarly, removing a fitment row sends an updated (smaller) compatibility list to eBay on the next sync.

note

eBay processes listing updates asynchronously. After Newclear sends the update, allow 5–15 minutes for the fitment data to appear on the live eBay listing.

Tips​

tip

Make sure your auto parts category is mapped to an appropriate eBay Motors category (not a general merchandise category). Fitment only works properly in eBay's Motors Parts & Accessories section. Check your category mapping in Settings > Categories.

tip

Test your first fitment listing by searching eBay while logged in with a vehicle set in "My Garage." The "Fits your vehicle" badge should appear. If it doesn't appear after 15 minutes, check the category mapping and that fitment rows exist on the product.
