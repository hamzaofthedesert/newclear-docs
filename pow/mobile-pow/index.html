Mobile POW

You can receive warehouse transfers using the Newclear mobile app on your Android device. The mobile POW experience mirrors the web version — with the added convenience of using your phone's camera as a barcode scanner.

Accessing POW on Mobile​

  1. Open the Newclear mobile app
  2. Tap the POW tab in the bottom navigation bar
  3. You'll see your list of pending transfers (same as the web version)

Selecting Your Seller​

Before receiving, select your Seller:

  1. Tap the Settings or gear icon in the POW screen
  2. Select your Seller from the dropdown
  3. Tap Save / Apply

This is the same requirement as the web version. See Selecting Your Seller.

Scanning Barcodes with Your Phone Camera​

The mobile app uses your phone's built-in camera to scan barcodes:

  1. Open the transfer you want to receive
  2. Tap the Scan button or the camera icon
  3. Point your camera at the product's barcode
  4. Hold steady — the app automatically detects and reads the barcode
  5. The item is added to the received list
tip

Good lighting is important for fast, accurate scans. Avoid scanning in low light or with glare on the barcode. If the camera struggles, try covering part of the barcode to help the scanner focus.

Same Flow as Web​

The mobile POW experience follows the same flow:

  • Browse transfers → select one → view invoice
  • Scan items one by one or use Create All for bulk
  • Save as Draft if you need to pause
  • Complete when done

All features available on the web POW are available on mobile:

  • Create All — Bulk Receive
  • POW Drafts
  • Bulk SKU Paste (via a paste field in the mobile app)

Why Use Mobile POW?​

AdvantageDetails
PortabilityWalk the warehouse with your phone — no computer or USB scanner needed
Built-in cameraPhone cameras scan barcodes quickly and accurately
Hands-freeSet phone in a stand or use one hand to scan while the other handles items
Real-timeSyncs instantly — changes appear in the web dashboard immediately

Duplicate Protection​

Mobile POW has the same duplicate protection as the web version. Scanning the same barcode twice correctly increments quantity rather than creating duplicate receives.

Troubleshooting Mobile POW​

Camera permission denied: If the app can't access the camera, go to your Android Settings > Apps > Newclear > Permissions > Allow Camera.

Can't see transfers: Pull down to refresh the transfer list. Check that you're logged into the correct company account.

Scan not matching items: Check if the barcode is for prod_sku or vendor_sku. The mobile app checks both. If still not matching, the product may not be in your catalog — use Create All or contact the sender.
