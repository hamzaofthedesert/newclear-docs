What is POW?

POW stands for Point of Warehouse — Newclear's system for transferring inventory between two separate Newclear company accounts. It's the digital equivalent of a B2B invoice and receiving process, built directly into the platform.

The Core Concept​

POW is designed for a specific scenario: Company A ships physical products to Company B. Both companies use Newclear. Instead of managing this transfer with paper invoices or spreadsheets, POW digitizes the entire process:

  1. Sender (Company A) — Creates a transfer in Newclear by selecting products from their catalog to send. This generates a digital transfer record (like an invoice)
  2. Recipient (Company B) — Opens the POW tab in their POS, finds the pending transfer, and receives the items by scanning barcodes or using Create All

When receiving is complete, the products are added to Company B's Newclear catalog with quantities updated.

Why POW Exists​

This workflow is common in:

  • Liquidation/resale networks — A central sourcing company buys pallets and distributes to multiple reseller companies
  • Wholesale/distributor relationships — A supplier (Company A on Newclear) ships to a retailer (Company B on Newclear)
  • Multi-location businesses — Different locations set up as separate Newclear companies transferring stock between them
  • B2B Newclear partnerships — Two businesses that formally connect via Newclear's B2B feature

Sender vs Recipient​

RoleWhat They Do
SenderCreates the transfer in Newclear with the list of items being shipped. Uses prod_sku to identify products.
RecipientReceives the transfer via POW tab. Products are matched using vendor_sku (which is the sender's prod_sku).
note

The sender's prod_sku becomes the recipient's vendor_sku when the product is received. This is why the vendor_sku field exists and why it's important to keep it accurate. See SKU & Barcodes.

What Happens After Receiving​

When a recipient receives a POW transfer:

  • If the product already exists in their catalog (matched by vendor_sku): Quantity is incremented
  • If the product doesn't exist in their catalog: A new product record is created with the sender's data as a starting point

POW Is Not a Regular Sale​

POW transfers are warehouse transfers, not retail sales. They don't go through the POS payment flow. The "price" on a POW transfer is the wholesale/cost price — it affects cost-of-goods tracking but isn't a consumer transaction.
