Subscription Plans

Newclear offers three subscription tiers to match the size and needs of your business. All plans are billed through Stripe and can be upgraded or downgraded at any time.

Free Trial​

All new accounts start with a free trial — no credit card required. During the trial you get access to the full feature set so you can test everything before committing.

The trial period is shown in your dashboard and Settings > Billing. You'll receive an email reminder before it ends.

tip

Use the trial period to import your products, connect at least one channel, and process a test sale. This gives you a real feel for whether Newclear fits your workflow before you subscribe.

What Happens When the Trial Ends?​

When your free trial expires:

  • Your data is preserved — nothing is deleted
  • Access to new features is restricted until you subscribe
  • You can still log in and view existing data
  • To resume full functionality, go to Settings > Billing and select a plan
warning

Don't wait until the last day. If your trial ends during a busy selling period, channel listings may pause and POS functionality may be limited until you subscribe.

Upgrading Your Plan​

  1. Go to Settings > Subscription & Billing
  2. Click Upgrade Plan or Change Plan
  3. Select the new tier
  4. Confirm payment via Stripe

Upgrades take effect immediately — new features unlock right away. You'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing period.

Downgrading Your Plan​

  1. Go to Settings > Subscription & Billing
  2. Click Change Plan
  3. Select the lower tier
  4. Confirm

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. You keep full access until then. If the lower plan doesn't support a feature you're using (e.g., more channels than allowed), those features will become read-only until you remove the excess or upgrade again.

warning

Before downgrading, review what's included in the target plan. Losing channel access could pause your live listings.

Billing & Payment​

  • Billing is handled by Stripe — industry-standard secure payment processing
  • Accepted: all major credit and debit cards
  • Invoices are emailed automatically each billing period
  • View past invoices in Settings > Billing > Invoice History

Cancelling Your Subscription​

  1. Go to Settings > Subscription & Billing
  2. Click Cancel Subscription
  3. Confirm cancellation

After cancellation, your subscription remains active until the end of the paid period. Your data is retained for a grace period after that — contact support if you need to export before deletion.

note

Cancelling doesn't delete your account immediately. You'll have time to export your data after cancellation.

Need Help Choosing?​

If you're unsure which plan is right for your business, start with the free trial and reach out to support. The team can help recommend the right tier based on your product volume, channel count, and team size.
