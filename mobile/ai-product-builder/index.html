AI Product Builder (Mobile)

The AI Product Builder on mobile is the fastest way to add new products to your catalog. Scan a barcode, take a few photos, and the AI fills in the product details — you review and save.

What the AI Builder Does​

The mobile AI Product Builder combines three features in one flow:

  1. Barcode scan → triggers PRE lookup to auto-fill known products
  2. Photo capture → attach real product photos immediately
  3. AI Review → AI fills in description, title improvements, and any missing fields

The result: a complete product added to your catalog in under 2 minutes per item.

Accessing the AI Builder​

Tap the ✨ (sparkle) icon in the mobile app's bottom navigation bar. This opens Screen 1 of the AI Builder flow.

Screen 1 — Details (Scan or Enter)​

The first screen is where you identify the product:

Option A: Scan a Barcode​

  1. Tap the Scan icon (camera icon)
  2. Point your phone camera at the product's barcode
  3. The app detects the barcode automatically
  4. PRE (Product Research Engine) looks up the UPC — see Barcode Lookup (PRE)
  5. If found: product fields pre-fill with title, brand, description, images, MSRP
  6. If not found: fields remain blank for you to fill

Option B: Type Manually​

If you don't have a barcode or the scan isn't working:

  1. Type the product name in the Title field
  2. Fill in any other known details (price, condition, category)
  3. Skip the barcode field or type the UPC if you know it

Fields on Screen 1​

  • Title (required)
  • UPC/Barcode
  • Condition
  • Category
  • Selling Price
  • Cost
  • MSRP
  • Seller

Tap Next when ready.

Screen 2 — Photos​

The second screen is for taking or adding product photos:

  1. Tap Take Photo to use your phone's camera
  2. Take the photo — it appears in the photos grid
  3. Take additional photos (front, back, detail shots, etc.)
  4. Drag to reorder if needed
  5. Tap Next when done (you can skip this step, but photos help the AI and are required for most channel listings)
tip

Natural light produces the best product photos on mobile. Step outside or near a window. Avoid overhead fluorescent lighting which creates color casts.

Screen 3 — AI Review​

This is where the AI does its work:

  1. The app sends your title, description (if entered), photos, and UPC to the AI
  2. A loading indicator appears ("AI is reviewing your product...")
  3. Within 10–20 seconds, the AI returns:
    • An improved or enriched title
    • Full product description
    • Suggested tags
    • Any additional fields it can determine

You see the AI's suggestions in an editable form. Review each field:

  • Accept the AI's suggestion (leave it as is)
  • Edit anything that's wrong
  • Manually fill in anything the AI left blank

Loading State​

During AI processing, a subtle animation or loading spinner is shown. Don't navigate away — wait for the AI to complete before proceeding.

Saving to Catalog​

When you're happy with the product data:

  1. Tap Save or Add to Catalog
  2. The product is created in Newclear immediately
  3. It appears in your Products list (on web and mobile)

The product is saved with Status = Active by default, so it's immediately eligible for channel listings.

Tips​

tip

Use the AI Builder for all new product intake — especially for liquidation pallets. Work through items one by one: scan → snap 3 photos → review AI → save. A trained employee can process 20–30 products per hour with this flow.

tip

The AI Builder and PRE together are most powerful for branded consumer goods. For niche industrial or private-label items, expect to fill in more fields manually.
