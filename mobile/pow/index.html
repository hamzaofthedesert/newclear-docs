Mobile POW

Receive inventory transfers from other Newclear accounts directly from your phone — same as the web POW experience, but with your phone camera as the barcode scanner.

What is POW?​

POW (Point of Warehouse) is Newclear's inter-account inventory transfer system. When another Newclear company sends you products, they create a "transfer invoice." You use POW to receive those products into your own catalog.

See What is POW? for a full explanation of how transfers work.

Accessing POW on Mobile​

  1. Open the Newclear app
  2. Tap the POW tab in the bottom navigation (warehouse icon)
  3. Your pending incoming transfers are listed here

Receiving a Transfer​

Step 1 — Select a Seller​

Before receiving any products, you must choose which seller in your account the products will be assigned to:

  1. Tap Settings (gear icon) on the POW screen
  2. Select your seller from the list
  3. Tap Confirm
Don't skip this step

If you don't select a seller, the receive will fail or products will be assigned incorrectly. Always set the seller first.

Step 2 — Open the Transfer​

Tap the pending transfer you want to receive. You'll see the full invoice: sender info, product list, quantities.

Step 3 — Scan or Bulk Receive​

Scan one at a time:

  • Point your phone camera at each product's barcode
  • The app matches it to an item on the invoice and marks it received
  • Repeat for each item

Receive all at once:

  • Tap Create All to process the entire invoice in one step
  • New products get created in your catalog; existing products get their quantity incremented
  • You'll see a summary: "X products created, Y updated"

Duplicate Protection​

The same duplicate protection from web applies on mobile. If you tap Create All twice, the second request is blocked — you won't get double inventory. It's safe to retry if you're unsure whether the first attempt went through.
